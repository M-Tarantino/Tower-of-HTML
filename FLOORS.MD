## Skill Progression

```
Beginner (0-8)
├─ Understanding tags & closing tags
└─ Basic page structure

Intermediate (9-28)
├─ Content formatting (text, headings)
├─ Media & links
└─ Basic interactivity

Advanced (29-48)
├─ Content organization (div, span, lists)
├─ Forms & tables
└─ Attributes (class, id)

Expert (49-51)
├─ DOCTYPE & meta information
└─ Complete webpage integration
```

- **No storage required** — codes are human-readable and portable
